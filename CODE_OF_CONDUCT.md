# Code of Conduct

We are committed to creating a welcoming and inclusive community.

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
By participating, you agree to uphold these standards of respectful and
collaborative behavior.

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Avoid harassing or exclusionary behavior.
- Accept constructive feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue or
contact the maintainers.

For full details of the Contributor Covenant, see:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
